# Which layout do you want for the member and insurer screens?

Decided: 2026-11-04 · Case: c_5f2902fb-530f-42a7-945d-f9b847904738

## Question and choice

I showed four layouts. The owner chose "Wide ticket": "one landscape ticket, with the tear from top to bottom. The insurer sees one wide ticket for each member."

The owner added two changes: "let's rework the title though", and on "0.1 NEAR paid": "why NEAR paid? can we use tech agnostic, non crypto terms? with /copywriting".

## Why

- The owner did not like the earlier layout, and chose this one from the four.
- My reason: all of the member view fits on the first screen, and the ticket stays nearest to the paper ticket in the guidelines.

## Constraints

- The member sees one landscape ticket, with a vertical tear between "Yours to keep" and "All your insurer sees".
- The insurer sees one wide ticket for each member.
- First-view words are tech agnostic, with no crypto terms: no "NEAR", "testnet" or token amount.
- Technical names and the exact amount stay in Technical details, under Verified, because judges check them.
- Demo data and test money stay labelled on each screen, in plain words.
- All facts in `PRODUCT.md` stay true, and the checks and limits in Verified stay.

## Ruled out

- "The wall": the owner did not choose it. It replaced the torn ticket shape.
- "Ticket on ink": the owner did not choose it. A dark page can remind people of crypto apps.
- "Cover story": the owner did not choose it. The headline took half of the first screen.

## Comments

- "let's rework the title though": I wrote new titles, and I send them to the owner to choose.
- "why NEAR paid? can we use tech agnostic, non crypto terms? with /copywriting": I ran the copywriting skill, and I changed the first-view words.

## Open questions

- None. The owner chose the titles: see `docs/decisions/2026-11-04-screen-titles.md`.
