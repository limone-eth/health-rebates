# Which first line do you want on the insurer screen?

Decided: 2026-11-04 · Case: c_eccc8404-f861-4591-a23d-77f0ad84c4b9

Changes the insurer title in [the titles record](2026-11-04-screen-titles.md). The member title does not change.

## Question and choice

The owner asked: "why insurer title is the same?" Both titles started with "Health that pays,".

The owner chose: "Reward healthy customers, / without holding health data."

## Why

- The owner asked for the benefit "on getting cheap customers".
- My reasons: the line names the customers that the insurer wants, and the demo shows this action. It makes no claim about costs.

## Constraints

- The title does not say that the demo lowers claim costs. The demo does not prove this.
- The second line stays "without holding health data."
- The member title stays "Health that pays," / "without giving up your data."

## Ruled out

- "Keep healthy customers,": the demo does not show that customers stay.
- "Pay for healthy weeks,": it names no business benefit.
- "Reward for health,": it reads less naturally.

## Comments

- "on getting cheap customers": I found no earlier record of this benefit, in the repo or in this session. I told the owner.

## Open questions

- Product name. "Good Week" is only a placeholder.
